Roadmap

From one agreement to the whole book.

Where Mobilis is today, and the path from a sandbox to a custodian's whole book on Canton MainNet.

  1. Oct 2026 · Built

    The rulebook MVP

    On-ledger valuation with custodian price marks, coverage, concentration limits and settlement re-checks, a margin-call lifecycle, returns, the collateral optimiser with top-up and return suggestions, the regulator report, 20 ledger tests and 16 shared parity cases.

  2. Q4 2026

    DevNet

    Done: the move to Canton 3.x and the JSON Ledger API v2 (all tests and the full live scenario pass on 3.5). Wallet connect through the Canton dApp SDK is in. Next: real authentication, a parallel-run (shadow) mode, and one agreement running across separate participant nodes.

  3. Q1 2027

    Design-partner pilot

    One custodian and one of its pledgor clients. A market-data feed behind the custodian's price marks, tokenised holdings via the Canton token standard, cycle time measured against today's process.

  4. Q2 2027

    Portfolio scale

    Many agreements per custodian, netting across agreements, intraday margin, and portfolio-level optimisation.

  5. H2 2027

    Production

    MainNet, Featured App status, and more custodians and triparty agents.

Known risks, and our answer to each

Our own assessment of what could stop an ops team adopting Mobilis. These are not interview findings.

No ops team will do a hard cut-over

Mobilis runs in parallel first: it reads the same movements, checks them against the agreement and flags differences, before anything settles through it.

Disputes and failed settlements

Today the ledger refuses a bad move, and a counterparty can dispute a proposed call with a reason. Next comes a full dispute lifecycle (raised, evidence attached, resolved or escalated), and a Failed state for settlements with a reason and a retry.

The legal terms behind the numbers

A terms view that links every haircut, limit and threshold to its CSA or GMRA clause and version.

Plugging in custodians and triparty agents

Standard ISO 20022 import and export of settlement instructions and positions, plus a reconciliation feed, so existing tools keep working without custom builds.

A screen too sparse for an ops desk

A dense blotter across agreements: pledged versus available by asset, pending items, and filters.

Data residency and audit trail

Each firm runs its own Canton node and holds only its own data, and every action is attributable. Before any pilot we document where data sits and how an audit export works.

Pilot shape: one asset class, one counterparty relationship, run in parallel with today's process. A plan, not a promise. Dates after October 2026 depend on the pilot partner. Full detail in docs/ROADMAP.md.
