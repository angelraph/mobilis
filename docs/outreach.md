# Interview outreach kit

Goal: 5 to 8 calls of 15 minutes each by Oct 7, logged in
[validation.md](validation.md). Send about 25 to 30 messages to get there.
Reply rates on cold messages are low, so send them all on day one.

## Who to find (LinkedIn search terms)

- "collateral management" + custodian / triparty / securities services
- "margin operations", "collateral operations", "securities financing operations"
- "repo desk", "treasury operations" at dealers or asset managers
- "regulatory reporting" + securities finance
- Canton ecosystem: the HackCanton Discord and Telegram, mentors, validator operators

Best targets are analysts to heads of operations, not C-level. They run
the process day to day and are more likely to reply.

## LinkedIn connection note (under 300 characters)

> Hi [Name], I'm building Mobilis, a tool that checks collateral
> eligibility, haircuts and coverage automatically on a shared ledger.
> I'd value 15 minutes of your view on how substitutions work at [firm
> type] today. No pitch, just learning. Open to a quick call this week?

## Direct message (after they accept)

> Thanks for connecting, [Name]. Quick context: Mobilis is a HackCanton
> project that puts the collateral rulebook (eligibility, haircuts,
> coverage, concentration limits) on the Canton ledger, so a substitution
> can't be agreed if it breaks the agreement.
>
> I'm trying to learn how this really works today before I build further.
> Could I ask you 5 questions in a 15-minute call, any time before
> [date]? Happy to share what I learn from the other conversations too.
>
> Have a look if useful (a 3-minute demo is on the site): https://mobilis-angelraphs-projects.vercel.app

## Follow-up (3 days later, once)

> Hi [Name], one quick follow-up. Even a written reply works: roughly how
> long does a collateral substitution take at your firm, from request to
> settled, and where does it usually get stuck? Thanks either way.

## HackCanton Discord / Telegram post

> Building Mobilis (RWA track): collateral mobility where the ledger
> enforces eligibility, haircuts, coverage and concentration limits.
> Looking for anyone who has worked in collateral, repo, margin or custody
> operations for a 15-minute chat. Also keen to hear from Canton
> participants working near securities finance. DM me.

## On the call

Use the 6-question script in [validation.md](validation.md). Ask before
quoting anyone, even anonymously. Log each call the same day.

## Short asks (Oct 7): point people at the feedback page

The feedback page (https://mobilis-angelraphs-projects.vercel.app/feedback.html)
takes about a minute, with no call needed. Log every real answer in
docs/validation.md; never write one up that didn't happen.
