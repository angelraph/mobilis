# ICP — Ideal Customer Profile

## 1. Who they are

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Segment** | Securities-financing and collateral operations: custodians, triparty agents, and the dealers and asset managers who post collateral through them |
| **Company size / stage** | Mid-size custodians and triparty agents, and their institutional clients, that want a low-risk pilot before rebuilding core systems |
| **User** | Collateral / margin operations analysts and managers (custodian); treasury and funding desks (pledgor); lenders and repo counterparties (secured party) |
| **Buyer** | Head of collateral operations or securities services at the custodian or triparty agent |
| **Geography** | Europe, UK and US first, where tokenised collateral work on Canton is most active |

---

## 2. Their pain

- **Top pain point:** "Every substitution means checking eligibility, haircuts, coverage and concentration on our own data, chasing the counterparty for agreement, and reconciling afterwards. Breaches show up after settlement, and the regulator report is assembled by hand."
- **How often it happens:** daily. Margin calls and substitutions are routine across every agreement.
- **What it costs them:** operations headcount, reconciliation breaks, disputes over valuations, operational and regulatory risk (industry estimate: €1.6–8B a year in manual processing).
- **How they solve it today:** spreadsheets, email and internal collateral systems that are reconciled against each other.

---

## 3. What they want

- **Job to be done:** "When a client asks to substitute collateral, I want the move checked and settled against the agreement automatically, so I can release the asset the same day without a reconciliation break or a breach."
- **What would make them switch:** a pilot on one agreement that shows fewer steps, no reconciliation, and breaches prevented rather than reported, without touching their core systems.
- **What would stop them:** compliance review, integration with existing collateral and pricing systems, trust in a new vendor, and whether their counterparties are on Canton.

---

## 4. Where to find them

- **Communities, events and channels they use:** Canton Industry Working Group on collateral mobility; securities-finance industry events and associations; LinkedIn (collateral management, margin operations, triparty); the HackCanton / AppsFactory partner network.
- **Tools and platforms they already rely on:** internal collateral management systems, pricing feeds, SWIFT messaging, and, for early movers, Canton-based platforms such as Broadridge DLR.

---

## 5. Who is NOT your customer (for now)

- **Retail users and DeFi traders:** no regulated collateral agreements.
- **Very large global custodians:** long procurement cycles; better approached after a mid-size pilot.
- **Firms with no margin or repo relationships:** no recurring collateral movements.
